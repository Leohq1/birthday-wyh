Candle Mechanics — How Should Blowing Work?

The microphone-driven candle interaction is the centerpiece

A

🕯️ One at a Time

Each blow extinguishes one candle. The user needs to blow multiple times (~5 blows for 5 candles). Slow reveal, builds anticipation. Each extinguished candle makes a small puff of smoke.

B

💨 All at Once (Sustained Blow)

User must blow continuously. A "wind strength" meter fills up. Once sustained long enough, all candles go out together in one satisfying whoosh. One dramatic moment.

C

🎯 Hybrid — Progressive with Finale

Candles blow out one by one as the user keeps blowing, with a short pause between each. After the last candle, a small celebration effect (sparkle burst). Natural, game-like feel.

Also: Microphone Fallback

Some devices don't have microphones, or the user might deny permission. What's the fallback?

🖱️

Click / Tap to Blow

A big on-screen "Blow" button. Tap/click rapidly, or hold, to simulate blowing. Simple and universal.

⌨️

Spacebar / Key Press

Hold spacebar or repeatedly press to blow. Good for desktop, not great for mobile.

👍

Both — Auto-Detect Best Option

Mic first (with a prompt). If denied or unavailable, show a big button that works for click AND spacebar.
