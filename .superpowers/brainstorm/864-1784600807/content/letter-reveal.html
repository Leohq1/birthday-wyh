💌 How Should the Letter Appear?

This is the emotional payoff — the moment after the candles go out

A

📜 Envelope That Opens

A 3D envelope floats down, the flap opens, and a paper letter slides out and unfolds. The text appears as if being written (typewriter effect). Very cinematic.

B

✨ Text Fades In Elegantly

Candles go dark, the scene softly dims, and the letter text fades in — either on a floating paper texture or directly as elegant text over the scene. Simple, clean, lets the words speak.

C

🎆 Scroll Unfurls from Above

A decorative scroll drops from the top of the screen and unfurls downward, revealing the message as it unrolls. Old-world romantic, like a royal proclamation of love.

Also: Does the letter replace the 3D scene or overlay it?

📌

Overlay — Scene Stays in Background

The cake remains visible in the background (dimmed), with the letter floating in front. The 3D world is still there, softly animated.

🔄

Replace — Full Screen Letter

The 3D scene transitions away and the letter takes over the full screen. More dramatic, like turning the page to a new chapter.
