Flowers — Arrangement & Style

How should the flowers appear in the scene?

A

🌹 Scattered Around the Cake

Individual 3D flowers (roses, tulips, or daisies) placed on the "table" surface around the cake. Feels like a decorated birthday table. Each flower is its own 3D object.

B

💐 A Bouquet Beside the Cake

One main bouquet of flowers sitting next to the cake. More dramatic focal point, easier to make look good since flowers cluster together.

C

🌸 Petals & Floating Flowers

Flower petals gently falling from above, plus a ring of flowers circling the base of the cake. More magical/romantic atmosphere — animated particles and soft motion.

D

✨ All of the Above

Bouquet, scattered flowers, AND floating petals. Maximum visual richness but more complex to build and heavier to render.
